# :wave: About
This repository contains the code and data for the paper: "Spatial and ecological determinants for community-based management of Amazonian fisheries"

# :page_facing_up: Paper and citing
Emer, C., Lurgi, M., Timóteo, S., Campos-Silva, J. V., & Pilosof, S. (Year). Spatial and ecological determinants for community-based management of Amazonian fisheries. PREPRINT INFO

# Abstract:
The increasing global demand for natural resources poses significant challenges to the sustainability of small-scale fisheries. Community-Based Management (CBM) has emerged as a vital strategy to maintain viable fish populations while mitigating the pressures of market demand and illegal fishing. In this study, we modelled the effects of various CBM scenarios on the abundance of Arapaima (Arapaima gigas) populations, a crucial income source for Amazonian communities, across 13 protected and 18 unprotected lakes in the Middle Juruá River Basin, Western Brazilian Amazon. Current CBM is suboptimal at enhancing Arapaima populations in unprotected lakes. CBM based on lakes’ carrying capacity promotes equitability between protected and unprotected lakes, maximizing source-sink dynamics. Geography and network topology scenarios favoured Arapaima population at low fishing pressure. These findings underscore the complexity of achieving sustainable Arapaima fishing, where the effectiveness of CBM depends intricately on protection criteria and fishing intensity.
